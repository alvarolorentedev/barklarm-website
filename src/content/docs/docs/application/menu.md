## Summary Icon

The Barklarm icon in the macOS menu bar or desktop system tray summarizes the status of your configured observers.

## Menu

Open the Barklarm tray menu to see each observer. Expand an observer’s entry to follow its link, or choose **Configure** to open the [Observers window](/docs/application/configuration-window). Choose **Quit** to close Barklarm.

## Legend

- **Succeeded:** The most recent check passed.
- **Failed:** The most recent check failed.
- **Checking:** Barklarm is refreshing the observer’s status.
- **Unavailable:** Barklarm could not read a status for the observer.
